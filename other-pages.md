## Breadcrumbs

Breadcrumbs show where a page sits in your store, for example **Home › Women › Pace Runner**, and help visitors go back one level. They also tell search engines how your store is organized.

The **Breadcrumbs** section can be added to product, collection, collections list, blog, article, page and search templates. Place it first, above the main section. When visitors open a product from a collection, the breadcrumbs lead back to that collection.

## Pages

Pages such as "About us" or "Shipping" use the **Page** section.

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Heading size | Display, Extra large or Large. |
| Content width | Narrow or Normal. Narrow is easier to read for long texts. |
| Alignment | Left or Center. |

Write the page content in your Shopify admin, under **Online Store > Pages**. To add images, FAQs or other sections to a page, add sections to its template in the theme editor.

## Contact page

Create a page in **Online Store > Pages** and choose the **contact** template. It shows the page content, a **Contact form** section and an **FAQ** section you can fill with your most common questions, or remove. See [Contact form](sections/storytelling.md#contact-form).

## Password page

Shown to visitors while your store is password protected, in **Online Store > Preferences**. In the theme editor, choose **Password** in the page selector.

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Heading | For example "Opening soon". |
| Background image | Optional image behind the content. |
| Show newsletter signup | Lets visitors leave their email to hear when you open. |
| Button label | The label of the signup button. |

Visitors enter the password from a button on the page. As the store owner, you can log in from the same page.

## 404 page

Shown when a page doesn't exist. It has a clear message, a search field and a link back to your products.

## Gift card page

The page customers receive with a gift card. It shows the gift card value and code, with buttons to copy the code, add it to Apple Wallet and print it, and a QR code to use in your physical store.

To let customers send gift cards to someone else, turn on **Show recipient form for gift cards** in the **Buy buttons** block of the product page.
