## Contact support

{: .note }
The support contact form will be available here soon.

You get an automatic confirmation by email when your request is received, and a reply within **two business days**.

## Before you contact us

Many answers are already in this guide. Before writing:

1. Search this documentation, and read the [FAQ](faq.md).
2. Check that you are using the latest version of Sinew.
3. If something stopped working after you installed an app or edited the code, try the same page on an unmodified copy of Sinew, or with the app turned off. This tells us whether the problem comes from the theme.

## What to include

To help us answer quickly, include:

- Your store URL, for example `your-store.myshopify.com`.
- The page where the problem happens, and the steps to see it.
- What you expected, and what happens instead.
- Screenshots or a short screen recording.
- Your device and browser, for example "iPhone 15, Safari".

If we need to look at your theme settings, we may send you a collaborator request. You can approve it, and remove it afterwards, in your Shopify admin under **Settings > Users**. We never ask for your password.

## Support policy

### What support covers

- Help with the theme's features and settings, as described in this documentation.
- Questions about setting up your store with Sinew.
- Bugs in the theme's code. Critical bugs are fixed as quickly as possible and released as a theme update.

### What support doesn't cover

- Code changes and custom features, including code added in Custom Liquid.
- Themes whose code has been edited. We can help when the problem also happens on an unmodified copy of Sinew.
- Third-party apps and their code. We help you find out whether a problem comes from the theme or from an app; the app's developer supports the app.
- Store setup outside the theme, such as payments, shipping, taxes, domains and Shopify plans: Shopify Support helps with these.
- Design services, content creation, photos and 3D models.

### Response times and languages

- We reply within two business days, Monday to Friday, Central European Time, except public holidays in Italy.
- Support is available in English and Italian.

### Updates

Theme updates are free and are released through Shopify. See [How do I update Sinew?](faq.md#how-do-i-update-sinew)

### Purchases and refunds

Theme purchases and refunds are handled by Shopify, under the Shopify Theme Store terms.
