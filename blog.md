# Blog
{: .no_toc }

Write your posts in your Shopify admin, under **Online Store > Blog posts**. The theme controls how the blog and its articles look.

1. TOC
{:toc}

## Blog page

The list of posts of a blog. In the theme editor, choose **Blogs** in the page selector.

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Posts per page | 6 to 30. |
| Feature the first post | Shows the newest post larger, at the top. |
| Show tag filter | Shows the tags of your posts as buttons, so readers can filter by topic, for example "Running" or "Strength". |
| Show image, date, author, excerpt | What each post card shows. |

## Article page

A single post. In the theme editor, choose **Blog posts** in the page selector.

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Show image | Shows the post's featured image. |
| Show author | Shows the author's name. |
| Show share button | Lets readers share the post or copy its link. |

### Workout details

Articles can show the details of a workout above the content (**Duration**, **Level** and **Equipment**) and a list of the products used, with add to cart buttons.

Each article usually needs its own details, so connect them to article metafields:

1. In your Shopify admin, go to **Settings > Custom data > Blog posts** and add a definition for each detail: for example "Duration", "Level" and "Equipment" as single line text, and "Workout products" as a list of products.
2. Fill them in on each blog post in the admin.
3. In the theme editor, open the article template and connect each **Workout details** setting to its metafield with the **Connect dynamic source** icon.

Articles without values don't show the details.

### Comments

When comments are turned on for the blog, in your Shopify admin under **Online Store > Blog posts > Manage blogs**, readers can comment below the article. If comments need approval, readers see a message after posting, and the comment shows once you approve it.

## Blog posts section

To show your latest posts on the home page or any other page, add the **Blog posts** section. See [Blog posts](sections/storytelling.md#blog-posts).
