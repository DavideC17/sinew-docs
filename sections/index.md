# Sections

Sections are the building blocks of your pages. To add one, open the page in the theme editor, choose **Add section** in the sidebar and pick it from the list. Most sections can be used on any page: the home page, product pages, collections, pages and blog articles.

Every section has a **Color scheme** setting (see [Colors](../theme-settings.md#colors)) and **Top padding** and **Bottom padding** sliders for the space above and below it. They are not repeated in the tables of the next pages.

## Motion and accessibility

Several sections animate as visitors scroll: Hero parallax, Zoom reveal, Scrolling words, Scroll statement, Scrolling text and Feature tour. For visitors who turn on reduced motion in their device settings, these sections show their content without movement, and autoplaying slideshows, announcements and videos don't start on their own. Videos always have a pause button.
