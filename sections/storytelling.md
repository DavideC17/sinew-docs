## Scroll statement

A large statement whose words light up line by line as visitors scroll, for example your brand promise.

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Subheading | Small text above the statement. |
| Text | The statement. Words in *italic* show as outlined letters. |
| Text size | Medium or Large. |
| Alignment | Left or Center. |
| Button label, Button link | Optional button below the statement. |

Without scroll animation support, or with reduced motion, the text shows in full color.

## Scrolling words

One word at a time in the middle of the screen. While visitors scroll, the section holds the screen and each word gives way to the next, between an optional line of text above and below, for example "Built for" ... "days".

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Text above, Text below | The fixed lines around the words. |
| Text size | Medium, Large or Extra large. |
| Alignment | Left or Center. |
| Transition | Fade, Fade and blur, or Slide up. |
| Scroll length | Short, Medium or Long: how far visitors scroll for each word. |

**Word blocks** (up to 8): one word each. Three to five words work best.

With reduced motion, or in browsers without scroll animations, the words show as a list.

## Scrolling text

A band of short texts or logos that scrolls continuously, with a pause button.

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Text size | Small, Medium or Large. |
| Speed | Slow, Normal or Fast. |
| Direction | Left or Right. |
| Separator | None, Dot or Star between items. |
| Tilt | -4 to 4 degrees, for a dynamic diagonal band. |
| Speed up while scrolling | The band moves faster while the page scrolls. |
| Pause on hover | Stops the band while visitors point at it. |
| Show borders | Lines above and below the band. |

**Blocks** (up to 12): **Text** (solid or outlined letters, optional link) and **Image** (a logo or icon with a transparent background). This section can also go in the footer group, as a band above the footer on every page.

## Testimonials

Quotes from customers, athletes or coaches, as a grid or carousel.

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Subheading, Heading | The title of the section. |
| Layout | Grid or Carousel. |
| Columns on desktop | 2 to 4. |

**Testimonial blocks** (up to 12): quote, author, author details (for example "Marathon runner") and an optional portrait.

{: .note }
Only publish testimonials from real customers, with their permission.

## Logo list

Brand, partner or press logos in an even grid.

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Subheading, Heading | The title of the section. |
| Style | Plain, or Tiles (each logo on a soft tile). |
| Logo height | 24 to 96 px. Each logo keeps its proportions. |
| Columns on desktop / on mobile | 2 to 8 / 2 or 3. |
| Logo alignment | Left or Center, for rows with fewer logos than columns. |
| Show logos in grayscale | Logos with a link take their colors on hover. |

**Logo blocks** (up to 24): image (a PNG with a transparent background, at least 400 x 120 px), name (read by screen readers and search engines) and optional link.

For a moving row of logos, use **Scrolling text** with Image blocks.

## FAQ

Questions that open one at a time.

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Subheading, Heading, Text | The introduction. |
| Layout | Heading next to questions, or Heading above questions. |

**Question blocks** (up to 20): question, answer and **Open by default**.

## Table

A table built in the theme editor, without code: a size chart, a comparison, specifications or shipping rates.

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Subheading, Heading, Text | The introduction. |
| Column 1 to 6 | Column headings. Empty columns are hidden. |
| Use the first column as row headings | Makes the first column bold and read as a heading by screen readers. |
| Measurements | **As written**, or **Entered in centimeters** / **Entered in inches** with a cm / in switch. Type numbers or ranges such as `80–84` in the unit you choose, and visitors can switch to the other unit. Other text stays as written. |
| Unit shown first | Same as entered, Centimeters or Inches. The visitor's choice is remembered on every table. |
| Width | Narrow or Wide. |

**Row blocks** (up to 30): one value per column. On small screens, the table scrolls sideways.

To show the table in the product page size guide, see [Size guide](../product-page.md#size-guide).

## Blog posts

The latest articles of a blog.

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Subheading, Heading | The title of the section. |
| Blog | The blog to show. |
| Posts to show | 1 to 9. |
| Layout | **Bento** balances itself for any number of posts, with a large first post from three posts on. **Grid** shows even cards. |
| Show image, date, author, excerpt | What each card shows. |
| Show view all link | Links to the blog. |

## Contact form

A form with name, email, optional phone number and message. Messages are sent to your store's contact email, set in your Shopify admin under **Settings**.

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Heading | The title of the form. |
| Show phone number field | Adds an optional phone field. |

Errors are shown next to each field, and visitors see a confirmation after sending.
