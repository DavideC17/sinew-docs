## Install Sinew

1. In your Shopify admin, go to **Online Store > Themes**.
2. Find Sinew in the Shopify Theme Store and choose **Try theme** to preview it in your store, or **Add theme** after buying it. The theme is added to your theme library, unpublished.
3. Choose **Customize** to open the theme editor. Your live store doesn't change until you choose **Publish**.

{: .tip }
Set up the theme while it is unpublished. When everything looks right, open the **...** menu next to it in **Online Store > Themes** and choose **Publish**.

## The theme editor

The theme editor has three areas:

- **Sections** (left sidebar): the sections of the page you are viewing, grouped as Header, Template and Footer. Choose **Add section** to add one, drag the handle to reorder, and use the eye icon to hide a section without deleting it.
- **Theme settings** (paint brush icon): settings that apply to the whole store, such as colors, fonts and product cards. See [Theme settings](theme-settings.md).
- **Preview** (center): the page you are editing. Use the page selector at the top to switch between the home page, products, collections and other templates.

Sections contain **blocks**: the heading, text, buttons or products inside a section. Choose a section to see its blocks, then **Add block** to add more.

Most settings that accept text, images or links also accept **dynamic sources**: choose the **Connect dynamic source** icon next to the setting to show a metafield instead of fixed content, so each product or article shows its own value.

## Setup checklist

Following this order avoids redoing work:

1. **Products and collections.** Add your products with photos, variants (for example Color and Size) and descriptions, then group them in collections. For color swatches, see [Color swatches](product-page.md#color-swatches).
2. **Menus.** In your Shopify admin, go to **Content > Menus** and build your main menu and footer menus. Nested links become drop-down or mega menus.
3. **Logo and favicon.** In the theme editor, open **Theme settings > Logo**.
4. **Colors and fonts.** Adjust the five color schemes and the two fonts in **Theme settings > Colors** and **Typography**.
5. **Header and footer.** Choose your menu, header style and footer content. See [Header and footer](header-and-footer.md).
6. **Home page.** Replace the preset images and text section by section. See [Sections](sections/index.md).
7. **Product page.** Choose the blocks you need and set up the size guide. See [Product page](product-page.md).
8. **Collection page.** Set up filters in the Shopify Search & Discovery app, then choose the grid and pagination. See [Collections and search](collections-and-search.md).
9. **Cart.** Choose drawer or page, and set your free shipping threshold. See [Cart](cart.md).
10. **Social media links.** Add your accounts in **Theme settings > Social media**.

## Images

Sinew loads every image at the size each screen needs, so you can upload large files. For sharp results:

| Image | Recommended size |
| --- | --- |
| Full-width images (hero, slideshow, zoom reveal) | 2400 px wide or more, landscape |
| Mobile images (hero, slideshow) | 1200 x 1800 px, portrait |
| Product photos | 2000 px on the longest side, the same ratio for every product |
| Collection images | 1600 px wide or more |
| Logo | PNG or SVG with a transparent background |
| Favicon | 32 x 32 px PNG |

### Focal points and image position

When a section crops an image, Sinew keeps its **focal point** in view. To set it, choose the image in the theme editor, then **Edit** and drag the focal point onto the most important part of the photo, for example the athlete's face. The focal point applies everywhere the image is used.

The **Hero** and **Shoppable image** sections also have **Image position** sliders, for images that need a different crop in one place only. See [Hero](sections/media.md#hero) and [Shoppable image](sections/media.md#shoppable-image).

## Languages

The theme's storefront text is translated into English, French, German, Italian, Portuguese (Brazil) and Spanish. To change a text, for example the "Add to cart" label, go to **Online Store > Themes**, open the **...** menu next to Sinew and choose **Edit default theme content**.
